Design Rationale for LED Conditions

The LED indicator conditions were defined after reviewing common weather interpretation guidelines and IoT visualization practices. In many meteorological services, rainfall is treated as the most explicit indicator of current weather, followed by strong wind conditions and clear weather signals.

Based on these references, rainfall amount (RN1), wind direction/speed (VEC, WSD), and humidity (REH) were selected as key parameters. The goal was to ensure that each LED state represents a clearly distinguishable real-world weather situation.
